<Language code="es">
En la oscuridad me busco” nace de la necesidad de entender quién es unx, dado que estamos tan inmersxs en entender el mundo exterior que ignoramos quiénes somos. Este búsqueda no resuelve en un camino lineal sino uno caracterizado por vueltas y mareos, lo que se intentó evidenciar mediante el movimiento de los cuerpos que se observan en la obra. En la oscuridad se dificulta la vista, por lo que esta nos empuja a la autorreflexión, a ir más allá de lo simplemente observable.

**Instrucciones de uso**

Tener prendida la WebCam, mover el mouse para cambiar la disposición de la luz, “S” para frenar la música, “Barra espaciadora” para reanudar la música.

<Language code="en">
In the darkness I look for myself" is born from the need to understand who one is, given that we are so immersed in understanding the outside world that we ignore who we are. This search does not resolve in a linear path but rather one characterized by twists and dizziness, which was attempted to be evidenced through the movement of the bodies that are observed in the work. In the darkness vision is difficult, which is why it pushes us to self-reflection, to go beyond what is simply observable.

**Instructions for use**

Have the WebCam on, move the mouse to change the light arrangement, “S” to stop the music, “Space bar” to resume the music.

**Biography**

**Facundo Molinas** was born in Mar del Plata. There he carried out his primary, secondary and even university studies; He studied Engineering and Economics before deciding on the path he really wanted to take: the arts. Throughout his life he was strongly interested in different branches of art, he studied music for much of his life and participated in numerous film and audiovisual courses. He left Mar del Plata to study Multimedia Arts at the University of the Arts, a career that mixes two of his passions: art and technology.
