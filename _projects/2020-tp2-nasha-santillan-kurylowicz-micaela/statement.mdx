<Language code="es">
La propuesta artística que se observa es una pieza de arte generativo, basada en el último EP de la cantautora española, Natalia Lacunza, el cual recibe el nombre de EP2 mismo que lleva esta obra. La intencionalidad de la misma no es más que dar a conocer a esta artista de un modo simple y entretenido, en una especie de homenaje. Mi trabajo es una obra de arte generativo porque hace uso de un código eficaz para su creación, aunque este no sea el centro de la propuesta. Lo que interesa de esta producción es el resultado final, la obra en su totalidad. Este tipo de arte utiliza el código como una herramienta más del proceso generativo. En cuanto a su funcionamiento, tiene una parte autónoma, como la mayoría de este tipo de propuestas, que es la lluvia de corazones que comienza de forma automática al iniciar la obra; cuenta también con una parte que debe ser iniciada por el espectador presionando las teclas L o Y

**Instrucciones de uso**

Presionar las teclas "L" o "Y", según lo desee, para reproducir el audio.

<Language code="en">
The artistic proposal that is observed is a piece of generative art, based on the latest EP by the Spanish singer-songwriter, Natalia Lacunza, which is called EP2, the same name that this work carries. The intention of it is nothing more than to make this artist known in a simple and entertaining way, in a kind of tribute. My work is a work of generative art because it uses an effective code for its creation, although this is not the center of the proposal. What is interesting about this production is the final result, the work in its entirety. This type of art uses code as another tool in the generative process. As for its operation, it has an autonomous part, like most of this type of proposals, which is the rain of hearts that begins automatically when the work begins; It also has a part that must be started by the viewer by pressing the L or Y keys

**Instructions for use**

Press the "L" or "Y" keys, as desired, to play the audio.

**Biography**

**Santillan, Nasha.** I was born on July 5, 1999 in Buenos Aires, Argentina. I lived in the town of Banfield until I was 6 years old, then I moved to the city of Berazategui, where I finished primary school and later moved to the province of Mendoza, more specifically to the city of Rivadavia, where I attended secondary school until 2017; The following year I began my degree in Electronic Engineering, which I left in 2019 to enroll at UNA for a Bachelor's Degree in Multimedia Arts, which culminated in a new move. I am a person who seeks and needs constant changes, which is why this career fits me very well since it gives me the possibility of generating them, by having such diverse proposals to develop. My approach to the arts does not date back long, it was in 2015 where I started a school workshop in Audiovisual Production and discovered a totally new and unique world for me.
