A collage with some of the works of the Norwegian artist Edvard Munch, a precursor of expressionism.

**Biography**

My full name is **Brian Javier Fuda**, I was born on March 3, 2000 (I am 20 years old) and I am from the Merlo party (Bs.As). I graduated from middle school number 16 in Merlo (formerly Manuel Belgrano) with a focus on visual arts in 2017. Since high school, the subject of animation in general has always caught my attention, which is why I decided to enroll in UNA Multimediales, to be able to delve deeper and specialize more in the subject.
