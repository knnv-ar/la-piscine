Multimedia Arts 1 & 4 — Lacabanne Chair

The project

This permanent (24/7) exhibition device sets into circulation the creative drive of the students during their coursework in Multimedia Arts 1 (cátedra Lacabanne), belonging to the Bachelor’s in Multimedia Arts at the Department of Multimedia and Electronic Arts of the UNA (Autonomous City of Buenos Aires, Argentina).

Far from being a mere repository, the gallery functions as a membrane where code takes on a body and expresses itself. <la-piscine> documents and exhibits the poetic and speculative journey of the students through creative writing in p5.js, forcing an intimate zone of contact between computational logic, aesthetic stamp, and the density of theoretical reflection.

The site you navigate today is the third mutation of the project. After shedding its initial configuration—Benjamin Habert's Ruby/Jekyll-based template—the project acquires a new technical corporeality: an infrastructure sustained by a native JavaScript stack, designed to house the urgency and continuous movement of these explorations, situated between classical propositional logic and the faltering cadences of desire.

We invite you to visit our official YouTube channel to watch student project defenses and tutorials.

Full Tenured Professor
Raúl Federico Lacabanne

Tenured Full Professor at the Universidad Nacional de las Artes (UNA). Tenured Associate Professor at the Universidad Nacional de Quilmes (UNQ). Professor for the courses "Multimedia Arts 1", "Multimedia Arts 4", and "Arts and Aesthetics of the 21st Century". Director of the UNA-PIACyT-LG 2026-2027 research project "Intimate Relations III: Care as a Critical Infrastructure in Contemporary Artistic Practices". Master's in Digital Arts (Universitat Pompeu Fabra). Bachelor's Degree in Electroacoustic Media Composition (UNQ).

Adjunct Professor
Mauricio Gutiérrez

Tenured Associate Professor at the Universidad Nacional de las Artes (UNA). Bachelor's Degree in Multimedia Arts from UNA. He produces artworks through experimentation across multiple artistic disciplines, materialities, formats, and media, focusing on both installation and performance practices. Since 2011, he has been part of an experimental art group called A.S.M.A Multimedia, alongside artists Sebastián Zavatarelli and Adelina Ducos. He has participated in various academic presentations, exhibitions, and festivals.

Assistant Professor (JTP)
Mirna Barreiro

Head of Practical Works for the course Multimedia Arts 4 in the Bachelor's in Multimedia Arts program at UNA. Bachelor's Degree in Music and Technology (UNQ) and Image and Sound Designer (UBA). She works professionally in the following areas: programming and electronics applied to art, web development, video post-production, animation using various techniques (motion graphics, programmed animation, stop motion, 3D), VJing, virtual set design, and musical composition and performance.
