Multimedia Arts 1 & 4 — Lacabanne Chair

The project

This permanent (24/7) exhibition device sets into circulation the creative drive of the students during their coursework in Multimedia Arts 1 (cátedra Lacabanne), belonging to the Bachelor’s in Multimedia Arts at the Department of Multimedia and Electronic Arts of the UNA (Autonomous City of Buenos Aires, Argentina).

Far from being a mere repository, the gallery functions as a membrane where code takes on a body and expresses itself. <la-piscine> documents and exhibits the poetic and speculative journey of the students through creative writing in p5.js, forcing an intimate zone of contact between computational logic, aesthetic stamp, and the density of theoretical reflection.

The site you navigate today is the third mutation of the project. After shedding its initial configuration—Benjamin Habert's Ruby/Jekyll-based template—the project acquires a new technical corporeality: an infrastructure sustained by a native JavaScript stack, designed to house the urgency and continuous movement of these explorations, situated between classical propositional logic and the faltering cadences of desire.

We invite you to visit our official YouTube channel to watch student project defenses and tutorials.
