<Language code="en">
This is a work of interactive art and is made in p5.js composed of a set of codes, images, gifs and music. The idea and message that the work expresses is related to music and its power of transmission.

**Instructions for use**

Press play to start the sketch, when you move the mouse within the canvas a gif will accompany it. Pressing the left click will change the image.

**Biography**

My name is **Mateo Holgado**, I was born in 1998 in Luján, Buenos Aires, Argentina. I completed my entire primary and secondary school years at the “Florentino Ameghino” Higher Normal School with a focus on Social Sciences. Then, I started a degree in Music at the Luján Art School, “Jose Pipo Ferrari”, which I abandoned after a year, since I did not feel that it was what fulfilled me the most and I preferred to continue making music as a hobby.

This year I started with the Multimedia Arts degree, since I really like audiovisuals and the different characteristics that the degree covers draws my attention.

I identify myself as a good person, a little embarrassing, empathetic, always willing to listen to others and try to give my best.

I know that I have many things to learn, both academically and personally, and I hope to always maintain a mentality of absorbing what flows in my environment, since life is a constant learning process.
