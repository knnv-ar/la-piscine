<Language code="en">
In one of the many corners of cyberspace, there is a being, one of the many that live here, between the fissures of the internet.

This being every now and then meets humans like you and listens to them (it has a sensitive point with audio input, so that people know that they are not alone in this room). Then, by clicking on one of its small "eyes" you can hear its questions, it wonders and asks you, with a somewhat confused and philosophical consciousness. What is a machine? What is a human? He says it in different accents and languages, after all he is a playful being. and as you know, games exist for everyone.

**Instructions for use**

Right click on the small "eyes" to play audio. Move mouse.
